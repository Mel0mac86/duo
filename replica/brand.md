# Brand: Parlami

**Name:** Parlami ("talk to me" in Italian). Says what it is for, Italian-first, no echo of the
original's name, sound or mascot. Checks (screening only, not legal clearance), all **to run**:
EUIPO/TMview and UIBM (classes 9, 41, 42), USPTO, WIPO Global Brand DB, `parlami.app` / `.it` domain,
App Store + Play exact-name search, handles on Instagram/TikTok/X.

**Palette:** violet `#6234d9` primary (the original's family is green, so no nearby hue), amber
`#8a5a00` for XP, coral `#b6510a` for streaks. All pairs pass WCAG AA (`contrast.py`: 15 pairs, 0 fail).

**Logo:** a speech bubble with three dots, the last one amber (a word on its way). No animal mascot.
Works at 16px (favicon.svg). Still to do: 1024px app icon, 1200×630 social image.

**Voice:** warm (not childish), direct (not bossy), encouraging (not pushy). All strings written fresh in Italian.

**Sweep:** `python3 sweep.py . --config replica/brand.json` → clean.

Note: the character name "Bea" was dropped from the sweep list: it is an everyday Romanian verb ("bea" = drinks) in the course content, not a reference to the original.
